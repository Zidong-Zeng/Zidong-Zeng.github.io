About Me
======
I am a senior undergraduate student majoring in Computer Science at Northeastern University (NEU), China.

Currently, I am a research assistant at ONE Lab. Advised by [Prof. Yao Wan](http://wanyaowang.github.io/).

My research interests lie in building more capable and adaptive LLM agents, with a focus on:

* **Long-Horizon Planning & Agentic Memory:** Developing structured and maintainable context and memory mechanisms to improve agents’ planning and execution over complex, long-horizon tasks.
* **Recursive Self-Improvement:** Enabling agents to learn from accumulated experience and continuously improve their planning, memory, tool use, and workflows over time.
* **Agentic Reinforcement Learning:** Exploring reinforcement learning methods for improving agent decision-making, planning, tool use, and long-term task performance through interaction and feedback.
